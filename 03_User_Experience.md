# 03_User_Experience

## Experience Goal

Visitors should feel like they are exploring a crafted world rather than
browsing a traditional website.

## Journey

1.  Arrive
2.  Understand the world
3.  Select a destination
4.  Travel
5.  Explore content
6.  Return and continue exploring
7.  Contact

## Interaction Principles

-   Click to travel
-   Immediate visual feedback
-   Smooth transitions
-   No unnecessary interruptions
-   Consistent interactions

## Navigation

-   Visitors choose where to go.
-   The ship travels automatically.
-   The camera follows the journey.
-   Content opens on arrival.

## Camera

-   Passive
-   Cinematic
-   Predictable
-   Never disorienting

## Animation

Animation communicates state changes, movement, focus, and completion.
It should never distract from content.

## Accessibility

-   Keyboard support
-   Reduced motion support
-   High contrast
-   Clear focus states
