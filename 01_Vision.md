# 01_Vision

## Purpose

Project Atlas is an interactive developer portfolio that replaces
traditional page navigation with exploration inside a persistent world.

## Vision

Create a memorable portfolio that demonstrates engineering skill through
the product itself while remaining intuitive, performant and accessible.

## Goals

-   Present professional work through exploration.
-   Encourage discovery without confusing visitors.
-   Balance creativity with usability.
-   Maintain production-quality code and performance.

## Experience Principles

-   Exploration over scrolling.
-   Motion with purpose.
-   Immediate clarity.
-   Fast interactions.
-   Accessible by default.

## Non-Goals

-   Not a game.
-   Not a clone of any existing fictional world.
-   No account system.
-   No backend services for v1.

## Success Criteria

Visitors should understand the project quickly, explore naturally, and
leave with a clear understanding of the developer's capabilities.
