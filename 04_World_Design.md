# 04_World_Design

## Concept

Atlas is a persistent world where destinations represent sections of the
portfolio.

## Principles

-   One continuous world
-   No page-based navigation
-   Exploration over scrolling
-   Every destination has a clear purpose

## Destinations

-   About
-   Projects
-   Skills
-   Experience
-   Contact

## Travel

Visitors select a destination and travel by ship. The world remains
consistent throughout the experience.

## Expansion

New destinations should be added without redesigning the world
structure.
